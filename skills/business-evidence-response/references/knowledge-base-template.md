## §3 Confidential Information Masking Rules

### §3.1 Items that MUST be masked for external responses

| Category | Masking rule | Example |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| Customer-specific terms | Replace with generic terms | "[Customer]'s format" → "the customer's format" |
| Internal form/document numbers | Redact or generalize | "[Form ID]" → "internal form" |
| Personnel names | Remove or generalize | "[Name]" → "authorized personnel" |
| Proprietary methods/processes | Summarize without detail | "[Specific process]" → "an established internal process" |
| Commercial/transaction terms | Remove completely | Pricing, volumes, contract details |
| Internal control numbers | Anonymize | "[Control #]" → "internal reference number" |

### §3.2 Items that should NOT be masked

| Category | Reason |
|----------|--------|
| Public standard references | Standards are public information (ISO, JIS, IATF, etc.) |
| General procedure descriptions | Without proprietary specifics, these are shareable |
| Internal document names for traceability | Needed for audit trail (if approved for external use) |
| General organizational capabilities | Broad descriptions of what the team/lab/dept can do |
